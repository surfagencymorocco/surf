Surf Camp Morocco from Italy

Italian surfers have discovered Morocco as their go-to winter surf destination, and it’s easy to see why. Direct flights from Rome Fiumicino, Milan Bergamo, and Bologna to Agadir take just 3 hours 30 minutes — less time than a train from Milan to Sicily. Morocco offers consistent Atlantic swell, warm water (16–21°C), and over 300 days of sunshine per year. EU citizens enjoy visa-free entry, French is widely spoken, and our all-inclusive 8-day surf camps start from just 550 EUR — incredible value for Italian surfers.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Africa’s longest wave with rides up to 800 metres. The mellow, predictable break at The Bay is a dream for beginners and longboarders. Fresh fish grilled at the harbour every evening.

Tamraght

Authentic Berber village set on a hillside with panoramic ocean views. Peaceful, traditional, and ideally located between the waves of Imsouane and Taghazout. Perfect for a relaxed surf holiday.

Taghazout

Morocco’s world-famous surf capital. Anchor Point, Killer Point, and Hash Point are all within a short walk. Cafes, rooftop terraces, and an energetic international surf scene.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from Italy

How long is the flight from Italy to Agadir?
Direct flights from Rome, Milan Bergamo, and Bologna to Agadir take approximately 3 hours 30 minutes. Connecting flights from other Italian cities like Naples, Venice, or Turin via a European hub add 1–2 hours.
Which airlines fly from Italy to Agadir?
Ryanair operates direct flights from Milan Bergamo, Rome Ciampino, and Bologna to Agadir. Air Arabia Maroc and Royal Air Maroc also offer direct and connecting services from major Italian airports.
Do Italian citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. Italian (EU) citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Morocco for stays of up to 90 days. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from Italy?
Our all-inclusive 8-day packages start from 550 EUR per person. Return flights from Italy to Agadir typically range from 80 EUR to 250 EUR. The total trip cost (flights + camp) is approximately 650–830 EUR, making Morocco substantially cheaper than long-haul surf destinations.
