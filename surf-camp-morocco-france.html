Surf Camp Morocco from France

For French surfers, Morocco is the closest world-class surf destination outside Europe. With direct flights from Paris Orly, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Toulouse, and Nantes to Agadir in just 3 to 4 hours, you can be in the water the same morning you leave home. No jet lag, no long-haul haul, and no visa required — EU citizens enjoy visa-free entry for up to 90 days. Our 8-day all-inclusive surf camps start from 550 EUR, covering accommodation, meals, daily surf coaching, equipment, and airport transfers.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Home to Africa’s longest wave. Gentle, forgiving rollers perfect for beginners and longboarders. Laid-back fishing village with fresh seafood at the harbour.

Tamraght

Authentic Berber village with panoramic ocean views. Consistent beach breaks for all levels. Close to Banana Beach, Crocodile Beach, and Devil’s Rock.

Taghazout

Morocco’s surf capital. Legendary point breaks including Anchor Point and Killer Point. Surf camp packages with guided sessions at the best spots.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from France

How long is the flight from France to Agadir?
Direct flights from Paris Orly take approximately 3 hours 30 minutes. Flights from Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, and Toulouse range from 3 to 4 hours. Nantes to Agadir direct takes around 3 hours 15 minutes.
Which airlines fly direct from France to Agadir?
Ryanair, Transavia France, Air France, and TUI fly all operate direct flights from multiple French cities to Agadir Al-Massira Airport (AGA). Royal Air Maroc also serves the route via Casablanca.
Do French citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. EU citizens (including France) can enter Morocco visa-free for stays up to 90 days. You only need a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from France?
Our 8-day all-inclusive surf camps start from 550 EUR per person. Return flights from France to Agadir typically cost between 80 EUR and 250 EUR depending on the season and how early you book. Total trip cost including flights and camp is approximately 650–800 EUR.
