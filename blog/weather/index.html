Morocco Surf Weather — Best Seasons, Swell & Water Temp

If you want to score good surf in Morocco, understanding the weather is everything. The Atlantic coast operates on a seasonal rhythm: winter delivers powerful North Atlantic swells that light up the point breaks, while summer offers smaller, cleaner conditions perfect for learning and longboarding.

Our weather guide breaks down month-by-month swell and wind patterns, water temperatures, the infamous Chergui (desert wind), and what wetsuit thickness you’ll need each season. Whether you’re chasing overhead barrels in January or mellow peelers in August, we’ll help you time your trip right.
