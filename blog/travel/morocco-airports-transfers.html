Agadir Al-Massira (AGA) — The Main Surf Gateway

Agadir Al-Massira Airport (code: AGA) is the undisputed best airport for a Morocco surf trip. Located 25 km southeast of Agadir city and just 45 minutes by car from Taghazout, it is purpose-built for the region and handles millions of passengers annually, the vast majority of whom arrive from Europe.

The airport has a single terminal that is modern, efficient, and easy to navigate. Facilities include currency exchange (open for all arriving flights), ATM machines, a small duty-free shop, a café, and car-rental desks (Hertz, Europcar, Avis, and local agencies). SIM card kiosks (Maroc Telecom, Orange, Inwi) are located in the arrivals hall and offer tourist data packages.

Pros of Flying to Agadir

  • Closest airport to the surf: 45 minutes to Taghazout and Tamraght, 90 minutes to Imsouane.
  • Direct flights from most European hubs: London, Paris, Warsaw, Berlin, Amsterdam, Brussels, Barcelona, and more.
  • Convenient transfers: Private transfers (â‚¬25-â‚¬35), taxis (â‚¬30-â‚¬40), or surf camp pickup (included in most packages).
  • Compact and efficient: You will be through immigration and collecting your board bag within 30-40 minutes of landing.

Cons of Flying to Agadir

  • Fewer long-haul routes: Almost all flights originate in Europe. Travellers from North America, Australia, or Asia will need to connect via Casablanca or a European hub.
  • Seasonal route reductions: Some airlines reduce Agadir routes in summer (June-September), though this is also the quiet season for surf.
Agadir Airport Is the Clear Winner

For 95% of surf travellers, Agadir is the right choice. The 45-minute transfer means you land at midday and can be in the water for an afternoon session. If your city has a direct Agadir route, book it and do not overthink it.

Marrakech Menara (RAK) — The Alternative Gateway

Marrakech Menara Airport (code: RAK) serves as a viable alternative, particularly if your departure city does not have direct flights to Agadir or if you are combining a surf trip with a visit to Marrakech. The drive to Taghazout takes about 3 hours via the A7 toll motorway, which is modern and well-maintained.

When Marrakech makes sense: You find significantly cheaper flights to RAK than AGA (savings of â‚¬100+ per person can justify the 3-hour drive). Your city has no direct Agadir route but does have Marrakech flights. You want to spend 1-2 days in Marrakech before or after your surf trip. You are travelling as a group and can share the cost of a private transfer or rental car.

Transfer options from Marrakech: Private transfer (â‚¬100-â‚¬140, 3 hours), rental car (from â‚¬25/day plus fuel and tolls), or CTM/Supratours bus to Agadir city (approx. 120 MAD / â‚¬11, 4-5 hours) plus taxi from Agadir to Taghazout (adds another hour and cost). Surf camps typically offer Marrakech transfers for â‚¬80-â‚¬120.

The Hidden Cost of Marrakech

A cheap flight to Marrakech is not always a bargain. Factor in the transfer cost (â‚¬100-â‚¬140 each way), the 3-hour drive time, potential overnight accommodation if you arrive on a late flight, and the tolls and fuel if driving yourself. Often the "more expensive" Agadir flight is actually cheaper when you account for the total door-to-door cost.

Essaouira Mogador (ESU) — Limited but Possible

Essaouira Mogador Airport (code: ESU) is a small regional airport with very limited international service. Flights are primarily from Paris (Ryanair) and a handful of seasonal routes from London and other European cities. It is approximately 150 km north of Taghazout, with a driving time of about 2.5 hours along coastal roads.

For most surf travellers, Essaouira is not a practical gateway to the Taghazout coast. It is only worth considering if you are specifically planning to surf the Essaouira area (Sidi Kaouki, Moulay Bouzerktoun) rather than the main Taghazout breaks. If Essaouira is your only viable flight option, arrange a private transfer (â‚¬80-â‚¬120) or rental car in advance — taxis at ESU are scarce and expensive.

Which Airport Should You Choose?

Here is the decision framework we use with our guests:

  • Fly to Agadir (AGA) if: Your city has a direct route. This is the answer for 95% of surf travellers.
  • Fly to Marrakech (RAK) if: You save â‚¬100+ on flights (per person), you are planning a Marrakech city visit, or there is no AGA route from your city. Accept the 3-hour drive as part of the experience.
  • Fly to Essaouira (ESU) if: You are specifically heading to Essaouira/Sidi Kaouki for surfing, or ESU is your only viable Moroccan destination.

Flights to Agadir — Routes by Country

Agadir is well-connected to Europe. Here are key routes as of the 2026 season (subject to change — always verify with the airline):

  • UK: London Stansted, London Gatwick, Manchester (Ryanair, easyJet, TUI)
  • France: Paris Orly, Paris Beauvais, Lyon, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Nantes, Marseille (Ryanair, easyJet, Transavia, Royal Air Maroc)
  • Poland: Warsaw Modlin, Warsaw Chopin, Krakow, Katowice, Wroclaw (Ryanair, Wizz Air)
  • Germany: Berlin, Cologne, Frankfurt, Dusseldorf (Ryanair, Condor)
  • Netherlands: Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Eindhoven (Transavia, Ryanair)
  • Belgium: Brussels Charleroi, Brussels Zaventem (Ryanair, TUI fly)
  • Spain: Barcelona, Madrid, Malaga, Valencia (Ryanair, Royal Air Maroc)
  • Ireland: Dublin (Ryanair)
  • Portugal: Lisbon, Porto (Ryanair)
  • Italy: Milan Bergamo, Rome (Ryanair)
Route Tip

Winter (October-March) is both the best surf season and the peak period for flights to Agadir. Airlines add extra frequencies and seasonal routes during these months. Summer routes are reduced but still available. Book 6-8 weeks ahead for the best fares.

Transfer Options from Each Airport

From Agadir (AGA) to Taghazout/Tamraght

  • Surf camp transfer: Included in most packages. Driver meets you at arrivals, 45 minutes door-to-door. The best option.
  • Pre-booked private transfer: â‚¬25-â‚¬35 online. Reliable, comfortable, often cheaper than a taxi. Recommended for independent travellers.
  • Airport taxi: 300-400 MAD (â‚¬30-â‚¬40). Official taxis at the rank outside arrivals. Agree price before departing.
  • Rental car: From â‚¬25/day. Collect at the airport, drive the N1 north. Useful if you want independence during your stay.

From Marrakech (RAK) to Taghazout/Tamraght

  • Private transfer: â‚¬100-â‚¬140, 3 hours. Book in advance — your accommodation or surf camp can arrange this.
  • Rental car: From â‚¬25/day. Take the A7 motorway south. Toll is approx. 70 MAD. The drive is straightforward in daylight.
  • Bus + taxi: CTM or Supratours bus to Agadir (120 MAD, 4-5 hours), then taxi to Taghazout. Cheapest but longest — budget 6+ hours total.

From Essaouira (ESU) to Taghazout/Tamraght

  • Private transfer: â‚¬80-â‚¬120, 2.5 hours. Arrange in advance — do not rely on finding a taxi at ESU.
  • Rental car: Limited options at ESU — reserve well ahead. The coastal road south is scenic but winding.

Booking Your Flights — A Timeline

8-12 Weeks Before

Start monitoring flight prices to Agadir (AGA). Set price alerts on Skyscanner or Google Flights. Identify which airlines fly your route and check their board-bag policies.

6-8 Weeks Before

Book your flights. Add your board bag at the time of booking — this is when it is cheapest and availability is highest. Book your surf camp or accommodation immediately after so your transfer can be arranged.

2 Weeks Before

Confirm your transfer or surf camp pickup. Save the driver's number in WhatsApp. Check your flight times — some airlines adjust schedules 2-4 weeks before departure.

48 Hours Before

Check in online. Confirm your transfer is still scheduled — send a WhatsApp message to your driver or camp. Download offline maps of the Agadir-Taghazout area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airport is best for a surf trip to Morocco?
Agadir Al-Massira Airport (AGA) is the best airport for a surf trip to Morocco. It is 45 minutes from Taghazout and Tamraght, and 90 minutes from Imsouane. It offers direct flights from dozens of European cities and is the closest airport to all major Atlantic surf breaks.
Is it better to fly to Agadir or Marrakech for surfing?
Fly to Agadir if you want the shortest, easiest transfer (45 min) and your city has a direct route. Fly to Marrakech if your city does not have direct Agadir flights, you find significantly cheaper flights to RAK, or you want to combine surfing with a Marrakech city break.
How far is Agadir airport from the surf spots?
Agadir Al-Massira (AGA) is 45 minutes from Taghazout and Tamraght, approximately 60 minutes from Agadir city beach breaks, and 90 minutes from Imsouane.
Can I fly to Essaouira for a surf trip?
Yes, but Essaouira Mogador Airport (ESU) has very limited international flights. It is 150 km north of Taghazout and is really only practical if you plan to surf the Essaouira area.
How much is a transfer from Marrakech to Taghazout?
A private transfer from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) to Taghazout costs approximately â‚¬100-â‚¬140 and takes about 3 hours via the A7 motorway. Surf camps typically offer Marrakech transfers for â‚¬80-â‚¬120.
Are there direct flights from the UK to Agadir?
Yes. Ryanair flies direct from London Stansted and Manchester. easyJet flies from London Gatwick. TUI fly operates seasonal routes. Flight time is approximately 3.5-4 hours.
Are there direct flights from Poland to Agadir?
Yes. Ryanair flies direct from Warsaw Modlin and Krakow. Wizz Air operates seasonal winter routes from Warsaw Chopin and Katowice.
What is the closest airport to Imsouane?
Agadir Al-Massira (AGA) is the closest airport to Imsouane at approximately 90 minutes by car. Essaouira (ESU) is about 1.5 hours away but has far fewer international flights.
How do I get from Agadir airport to Tamraght?
Tamraght is about 35-40 minutes from Agadir airport. A private transfer costs â‚¬20-â‚¬30. A taxi costs 250-350 MAD. Most surf camps in Tamraght include airport transfers in their package.
Is the drive from Marrakech to Taghazout safe?
Yes, the A7 motorway from Marrakech to Agadir is a modern, well-maintained toll road. It is safe during daylight hours. Driving at night is legal but adds stress.
Do I need an international driving permit for Morocco?
An International Driving Permit (IDP) is recommended alongside your home driving licence for car rental in Morocco. Some agencies accept standard EU/UK licences, but an IDP provides additional protection.
What is the cheapest way to get from Agadir airport to Taghazout?
A surf camp transfer is cheapest because it is included in your package. For independent travellers, a pre-booked private transfer (â‚¬25-â‚¬35) is the best balance of cost and convenience.
