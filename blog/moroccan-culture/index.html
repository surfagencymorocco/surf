Moroccan Culture Guide — Berber Traditions & Food

A surf trip to Morocco is about more than just waves — it’s an immersion into one of the world’s most vibrant cultures. The Berber (Amazigh) people have inhabited these coastal mountains for millennia, and their traditions of hospitality, storytelling, and craftsmanship are woven into everyday life in Taghazout, Tamraght, and Imsouane.

From the ritual of Moroccan mint tea to the flavours of tagine and couscous, from the weekly souk (market) to the traditional hammam, this cultural guide will help you connect with the people and places that make a Morocco surf trip unforgettable.
