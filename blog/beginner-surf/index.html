Beginner Surf Morocco — Learn to Surf in Africa

Morocco is one of the best places in the world to learn to surf. With warm water, consistent gentle waves, and hundreds of qualified instructors, the Atlantic coast offers ideal conditions for your first lesson, your first pop-up, and your first green wave.

From the mellow rollers of Imsouane Bay to the forgiving beach breaks of Tamraght, our beginner surf guide covers everything a first-timer needs: the best spots to learn, what gear to use, how to choose a surf camp, and how to prepare for your week in the water.
