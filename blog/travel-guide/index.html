Morocco Travel Guide — Flights, Visas & Getting Around

Getting to Morocco’s surf coast is straightforward. Agadir Al-Massira Airport (AGA) is the main gateway, with direct flights from dozens of European cities. From the airport, it’s a 45-minute drive to Taghazout or Tamraght and about 90 minutes to Imsouane.

This travel guide covers everything logistics-related: which airlines fly direct to Agadir, visa requirements (visa-free for EU, UK, and US citizens for stays up to 90 days), airport transfer options, local currency (Moroccan Dirham), SIM card providers, and how to navigate the coastal villages once you arrive.
