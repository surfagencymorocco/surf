Intermediate Surf Morocco — Progression & Coaching

So you can catch unbroken waves, ride down the line, and you’re hungry to progress. Morocco’s diverse coastline is the perfect classroom for intermediate surfers looking to refine their technique, improve wave selection, and build confidence on faster, steeper faces.

In this guide we cover the best intermediate spots between Imsouane and Taghazout, video analysis and coaching options available at local surf camps, and practical drills for improving your bottom turn, cutback, and positioning in the lineup. Morocco rewards surfers who are ready to step up — let us show you how.
