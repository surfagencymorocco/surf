Tamraght Surf Guide — Authentic Berber Surf Village Morocco

Perched on a hillside just north of Agadir, Tamraght is a traditional Berber village that has quietly become one of Morocco’s most beloved surf hubs. Unlike its busier neighbour Taghazout, Tamraght retains a genuine, slow-paced charm with narrow streets, family-run guesthouses, and panoramic views of the Atlantic.

The surf here is excellent and varied: Banana Beach and Crocodile Beach offer consistent peaks for all levels, while nearby spots like Devil’s Rock and Spider Beach provide more challenging waves. Our Tamraght guide covers where to surf, where to stay, and how to experience authentic Moroccan hospitality away from the crowds.
