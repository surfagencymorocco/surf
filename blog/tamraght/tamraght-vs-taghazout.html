Tamraght vs Taghazout — Which Surf Destination Is Right for You?

SurfAgencyMorocco Team July 21, 2026 12 min read
Photo: The view from Tamraght hillside looking north towards Taghazout, Morocco

Only five kilometres apart, Tamraght and Taghazout represent two very different versions of a Moroccan surf holiday. One is a lively, internationally famous surf hub with iconic point breaks, rooftop bars, and a constant hum of activity. The other is a quiet Berber village perched on a hillside, where the call to prayer sets the daily rhythm and the surf scene feels more intimate. Both offer excellent waves — but the experience of staying in each is markedly different.

Having lived and surfed in both villages, our team has put together this honest comparison. There is no single "better" option — only the one that matches what you are looking for.

Surf: Which Has Better Waves?

This question misses the point — because the two villages share the same stretch of coast. Every break accessible from Taghazout is also accessible from Tamraght (a 10-minute drive), and vice versa. The difference lies in what is on your doorstep.

  • In Taghazout, you wake up a 5-minute walk from Anchor Point. You can check the surf from your balcony, walk to Hash Point in flip-flops, and be in the water before the first tour vans arrive. For surfers obsessed with point breaks, this proximity is unbeatable.
  • In Tamraght, you wake up to panoramic ocean views from the hillside, drive 5 minutes to Banana Beach (or 10 minutes to Anchor Point), and surf whichever break is best that day. You trade doorstep point breaks for flexibility, variety, and quieter lineups.

Verdict: For pure surfing variety, it is a tie — you can access all breaks from either village. For point break fanatics, Taghazout wins on proximity. For those who want the full range (especially beginner-friendly waves), Tamraght has the edge.

For those who prioritise being in the centre of the action, Taghazout's premium is worth paying.

Atmosphere: Party Scene vs Peaceful Retreat

This is where the two villages diverge most dramatically. Taghazout in winter is buzzing — cafes are full, hostels host social events, and the rooftop bars stay lively into the evening. It is a classic surf town atmosphere: international, casual, energetic. If your ideal surf trip includes sunset beers, meeting people from a dozen countries, and a vibrant social scene, Taghazout delivers.

Tamraght is the opposite — peaceful, family-oriented, and deeply local. Evenings are spent on rooftop terraces with mint tea, watching the stars come out over the Atlantic. The loudest sound is the call to prayer echoing across the valley. If your ideal surf trip involves quiet, focus, and genuine cultural immersion, Tamraght is your place.

Pro Tip from Our Guides

Why not both? Start your trip in Taghazout for the social buzz and famous lineups. Then move to Tamraght for the second half — quieter, cheaper, and with equally good waves. It is a common pattern among repeat visitors: Taghazout for the energy, Tamraght for the soul.

When to Choose Tamraght

  • You are a beginner or improving intermediate who wants safe, sand-bottomed waves.
  • You are travelling with family or as a couple seeking a peaceful atmosphere.
  • You want the most authentic Moroccan cultural experience alongside your surfing.
  • You are on a tighter budget but do not want to compromise on wave quality.
  • You prefer quiet evenings on a rooftop to loud bars and late nights.
  • You want less crowded lineups and more personal coaching attention.

When to Choose Taghazout

  • You are an experienced surfer who wants to be walking distance from Anchor Point.
  • You want a lively social scene, rooftop bars, and international company.
  • You value a wide range of cafes, restaurants, and amenities steps from your accommodation.
  • You are a solo traveller looking to meet people easily.
  • You want to surf the famous point breaks at dawn without a 10-minute drive first.
  • You do not mind paying a premium for convenience and atmosphere.

"I started my surf career in Taghazout — it was exciting, social, everything a young surfer wants. After a few seasons, I moved to Tamraght and never looked back. The village is my home now. The waves are the same, but the peace... the peace is priceless."

— Rachid, SurfAgencyMorocco guide, Tamraght local

Decision Checklist

  • Are you a beginner? Choose Tamraght.
  • Do you want to walk to Anchor Point? Choose Taghazout.
  • Is budget a primary concern? Choose Tamraght (10–20% cheaper).
  • Do you want nightlife and social scene? Choose Taghazout.
  • Do you want peace, quiet, and authenticity? Choose Tamraght.
  • Travelling with kids? Choose Tamraght (family-oriented).
  • Solo traveller wanting to meet people? Choose Taghazout.
