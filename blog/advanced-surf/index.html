Advanced Surf Morocco — Pro Waves & Secret Spots

When the North Atlantic lights up, Morocco transforms into a heavyweight surf destination. Anchor Point on its day delivers world-class barrels, Killer Point breaks boards and egos, and the reefs around Tamri and Boilers produce hollow, powerful waves that rival anything in Europe.

This guide is for experienced surfers who can handle overhead-plus conditions, navigate reef breaks, and hold their own in competitive lineups. We cover swell forecasting, the best big-wave seasons, reef-specific advice, and the lesser-known breaks that rarely appear on public surf reports.
