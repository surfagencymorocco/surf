Taghazout Surf Guide — Anchor Point & Morocco’s Surf Capital

Taghazout is the undisputed heart of Moroccan surfing. Once a sleepy fishing village, it has evolved into a world-class surf destination that draws wave riders from across the globe. Anchored by the legendary Anchor Point — a long, peeling right-hand point break — this stretch of coast delivers consistent, powerful waves throughout the winter months.

Within a 10-kilometre radius you’ll find iconic breaks like Killer Point, Hash Point, and Panoramas, alongside countless lesser-known peaks. Our Taghazout guide covers the best time to visit, where to stay, which surf camps to book, and how to navigate the local lineups with confidence.
