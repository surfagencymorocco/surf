Best Areas to Stay — Bay, Harbour & Hillside

Imsouane is small — you can walk from one end of the village to the other in 15 minutes. But where you choose to stay still matters. The village divides naturally into three zones, each with a distinct character:

1. Near The Bay (South Side)

This is the surfer's choice. Accommodation here puts you 2 to 5 minutes' walk from the take-off zone at The Bay. In the morning, you can check the conditions from your terrace, grab your board, and be in the water faster than most surfers have finished their coffee. The south side has the highest concentration of surf camps, guesthouses, and surf cafés.

Pros: Closest to The Bay, surf-camp vibe, easy access to dawn patrol. Cons: Can be noisier (surf camp energy), slightly further from the harbour fish stalls.

2. Near the Harbour (Village Centre)

The harbour area is the beating heart of Imsouane. Here you will find the fish stalls, small grocery shops, and a handful of restaurants and cafés. It is equidistant from The Bay (10-minute walk south) and the Cathedral (10-minute walk north past the harbour). Staying here puts you in the middle of village life — you will fall asleep to the sound of waves and wake up to the calls of fishermen.

Pros: Central, close to food and amenities, good access to both breaks. Cons: The harbour can be lively (and fragrant) in the early morning.

3. Hillside (Above the Village)

The hillside rising above Imsouane offers the best views in the village — panoramic vistas of The Bay, the Cathedral, and the Atlantic stretching to the horizon. Accommodation here tends to be quieter, more spacious, and often includes terraces with unforgettable sunset views. The trade-off is a 10 to 15-minute walk down to the beach (and a slightly steeper walk back up).

Pros: Best views, quietest, more space, often better value. Cons: Uphill walk back from the beach, further from cafés and restaurants.

Our Recommendation

If you are here to surf (especially as a beginner), stay near The Bay. The 2-minute walk to the water in the morning versus a 10-minute walk makes a real difference when you are doing two sessions a day. If you are here with a non-surfing partner, the hillside offers the best views and a more romantic atmosphere.

Surf Camps — All-Inclusive Packages

Surf camps are the most popular accommodation choice in Imsouane, and for good reason. They bundle everything a surfer needs into one price: accommodation, daily surf sessions with an instructor, all equipment (board and wetsuit), meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), and airport transfers from Agadir. For beginners, this is by far the best option — you arrive, you surf, you eat, you sleep, and someone else handles the logistics.

A typical all-inclusive surf camp package in Imsouane includes:

  • Private or shared room in a guesthouse or riad
  • 2 surf sessions per day (morning and afternoon) with a qualified instructor
  • Surfboard and wetsuit rental for the duration of your stay
  • Breakfast, lunch, and dinner (traditional Moroccan cuisine)
  • Airport transfers from Agadir (AGA)
  • Optional extras: yoga sessions, video analysis, day trips to other breaks

Most camps offer packages of 7, 10, or 14 nights. Prices range from â‚¬50 to â‚¬90 per night depending on the quality of accommodation, instructor-to-student ratio, and season. Winter (December–February) is the most expensive; summer (June–September) can be 30–50% cheaper.

Guides' Advice on Choosing a Surf Camp

Look for camps with a maximum instructor-to-student ratio of 1:5. Anything higher and you will spend more time waiting for feedback than getting it. Ask about video analysis — camps that film your sessions and review the footage with you will accelerate your progress significantly. And always check what "all-inclusive" actually covers — some camps charge extra for lunch or equipment.

Budget Hostels & Guesthouses

If you are travelling on a budget, a solo surfer who does not need lessons, or a digital nomad looking for an affordable long stay, Imsouane's hostels and budget guesthouses offer excellent value. Dorm beds start at â‚¬10–15 per night, and private budget rooms start at around â‚¬20–35 per night.

Most hostels have shared kitchens, communal terraces, and a social atmosphere. They are popular with long-term travellers (1–4 weeks) and solo surfers. WiFi is usually available but can be slow, so bring a Moroccan SIM card with 4G data (Maroc Telecom has the best coverage in Imsouane) if you need reliable internet.

Keep in mind: budget guesthouses in Imsouane are basic. Hot water can be unreliable, rooms are small, and walls are thin. If you value comfort, privacy, or a good night's sleep, consider a mid-range riad or a surf camp instead.

Riads & Boutique Guesthouses

For travellers who want comfort, character, and a more authentic Moroccan experience, Imsouane's riads and boutique guesthouses are the sweet spot. Typically family-run, these properties offer private rooms (often with en-suite bathrooms), traditional Moroccan décor, and rooftop terraces with sea views. Breakfast is usually included — expect msemen pancakes, fresh fruit, orange juice, and mint tea.

Prices range from â‚¬35 to â‚¬80 per night, with the higher end offering larger rooms, better views, and more polished service. Many riads are located on the hillside, offering panoramic views of The Bay. If you are a couple, a small group, or an experienced surfer who does not need surf camp amenities (lessons, equipment, meals), a riad is an excellent choice.

Local Knowledge

The best riads in Imsouane are not on Booking.com — they are found through word of mouth, WhatsApp, or by asking your surf guide. Many of the best family-run guesthouses do not list on international platforms. If you know someone who has been to Imsouane, ask them for a direct contact. Alternatively, book a surf camp for your first trip and scope out riads for your return visit.

Apartment Rentals

For longer stays (2 weeks or more), renting an apartment is the most cost-effective option. Monthly rates range from â‚¬300 for a basic studio to â‚¬800+ for a two-bedroom apartment with a sea view. You will have your own kitchen, living space, and the freedom to cook your own meals (fresh fish from the harbour, local vegetables, and Moroccan spices from the small shops).

Apartments are particularly popular with digital nomads, remote workers, and long-stay surfers who want to settle in for a month or more. The best deals are found locally — walk around the village, ask at cafés, or check the noticeboards at surf shops. Online platforms like Airbnb have listings, but they are often 20–40% more expensive than local direct bookings.

Important: Check the WiFi situation before committing to a long-term apartment rental. Some apartments in Imsouane have no internet, and you will rely entirely on mobile data. If you work remotely, confirm WiFi availability and speed before booking.

Which Accommodation Type for You?

  • You are a complete beginner, never surfed before: Book an all-inclusive surf camp. You need lessons, equipment, and a structured environment. Trying to arrange everything independently as a first-timer will cost more and be far less effective.
  • You are an intermediate surfer who wants coaching and community: A surf camp is still your best bet — look for one with video analysis and a low student-to-instructor ratio.
  • You are an experienced surfer travelling independently with your own board: A riad or boutique guesthouse near The Bay. You do not need lessons, but you do want comfort, a good breakfast, and a short walk to the wave.
  • You are a solo traveller on a tight budget: A hostel dorm or budget private room. You will meet other travellers, save money, and still be minutes from The Bay.
  • You are a couple wanting a romantic surf trip: A hillside riad with a sea-view terrace. The walk to the beach is part of the charm, and the sunset views are worth it.
  • You are a digital nomad staying 1+ months: Rent an apartment. You will save significantly versus nightly rates, have your own kitchen, and settle into village life. Confirm WiFi before booking.
  • You are a family with children: A surf camp with family-friendly accommodation or a hillside apartment with multiple bedrooms. Check that the camp accepts children and has appropriate equipment (small wetsuits, foam boards).

Booking Tips

  • Book winter trips 6–8 weeks ahead. The best camps and riads in Imsouane are small and fill up fast during peak season (December–February).
  • Contact camps directly via WhatsApp. Many Imsouane surf camps offer better rates for direct bookings than through third-party platforms. A quick WhatsApp message can save you 10–15%.
  • Ask what is included. Not all "all-inclusive" packages are equal. Specifically ask: Are lunches included? Is equipment rental covered? Are airport transfers both ways? Is there a single-room supplement?
  • Read recent reviews (2025–2026). Imsouane's accommodation scene evolves quickly. A camp that was excellent in 2023 may have changed ownership or declined. Look for reviews from the last 12 months.
  • Bring cash (MAD). Many smaller guesthouses and riads in Imsouane do not accept credit cards. There is one ATM in the village, but it can run out of cash. Bring enough Dirhams to cover your stay, or withdraw in Agadir before driving up.

Accommodation Checklist

  • Decide your priority: proximity to The Bay, views, budget, or all-inclusive convenience
  • For winter (Dec–Feb): book 6–8 weeks ahead
  • For surf camps: confirm instructor ratio (max 1:5), video analysis availability, and what's included
  • For apartments/riads: confirm WiFi speed if you need to work remotely
  • Bring cash (MAD) — many small accommodations do not accept cards
  • Read reviews from the last 12 months — Imsouane's scene changes fast
  • Contact accommodations directly via WhatsApp for better rates
  • Check if airport transfers are included or need to be arranged separately

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best area to stay in Imsouane?
Near The Bay (south side) is best for surfers who want to walk to the wave in 2–5 minutes. Near the harbour (village centre) is best for access to restaurants, fish stalls, and the Cathedral. Hillside offers the best views and quietest atmosphere, but requires a 10–15 minute walk to the beach.
How much does accommodation cost in Imsouane?
Budget hostels and shared dorm rooms: â‚¬10–20 per night. Private rooms in guesthouses: â‚¬20–40 per night. Surf camp packages (all-inclusive): â‚¬50–90 per night. Boutique riads: â‚¬40–80 per night. Apartment rentals: â‚¬30–70 per night. Prices are 2026 estimates and vary by season — summer is 30–50% cheaper than winter.
Are surf camps in Imsouane all-inclusive?
Most Imsouane surf camps offer all-inclusive packages that cover accommodation, daily surf lessons or guided sessions, surfboard and wetsuit rental, all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), and airport transfers from Agadir. Some camps also include yoga, video analysis, and day trips to other breaks. Always check what is included before booking.
Should I book a surf camp or independent accommodation?
For beginners, a surf camp is strongly recommended — you get lessons, equipment, transport, and meals bundled into one price, which is usually cheaper than booking everything separately. For experienced independent surfers who have their own equipment and transport, a riad or apartment offers more flexibility and privacy.
Can I find budget accommodation in Imsouane?
Yes — Imsouane has several budget hostels with dorm beds from â‚¬10–15 per night. Private budget rooms start at around â‚¬20 per night. Prices are lowest in summer (June–September) when demand drops. The village is small, so even budget accommodation is within a 10-minute walk of The Bay.
Is there luxury accommodation in Imsouane?
Imsouane is not a luxury resort destination — it is a small fishing village. The most upscale options are boutique riads and surf camps with private rooms, sea-view terraces, and quality Moroccan cuisine. If you want true luxury (spas, pools, fine dining), Taghazout (30 minutes south) has more options.
Do accommodations in Imsouane have WiFi?
Most surf camps, riads, and hostels offer WiFi, but the connection can be slow and unreliable, especially in the hillside area. Mobile data (4G) via a Moroccan SIM card (Maroc Telecom or Orange) is often faster and more reliable for remote work. Do not expect high-speed fibre internet.
How far in advance should I book accommodation in Imsouane?
For winter (December–February): book 6–8 weeks ahead — the best camps and riads fill up. For shoulder seasons (March–April, October–November): 3–4 weeks ahead is usually sufficient. For summer (May–September): you can often book a few days ahead, but booking 1–2 weeks in advance secures the best options.
Can I rent an apartment in Imsouane for a month?
Yes — long-term apartment rentals are available, particularly popular with digital nomads and long-stay surfers. Monthly rates range from â‚¬300 for a basic studio to â‚¬800+ for a two-bedroom apartment with a sea view. The best deals are found by asking locally rather than booking online.
What's included in a typical surf camp package?
A typical all-inclusive surf camp package in Imsouane includes: accommodation (private or shared room), daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner, 2 surf sessions per day with a qualified instructor, surfboard and wetsuit rental, and airport transfers from Agadir. Some camps also include yoga sessions, video analysis, and transport to nearby breaks.
Are there any hotels in Imsouane?
There are no large chain hotels in Imsouane. Accommodation is almost entirely small, independently run guesthouses, riads, surf camps, and apartment rentals. This is part of Imsouane's charm — it has resisted large-scale development and retains the character of a traditional Moroccan fishing village.
Is it better to stay near The Bay or near the Cathedral?
If you are a beginner or longboarder, stay near The Bay (south side) — you can walk to the wave in minutes. If you are an intermediate or advanced surfer focused on the Cathedral, stay near the harbour (north side), which gives you access to both breaks and the village's restaurants. The distance between the two areas is only a 10–15 minute walk.
