Surf Guides Morocco — How to Surf the Atlantic Coast

Morocco’s Atlantic coastline stretches over 1,800 km and receives consistent North Atlantic swells year-round, making it one of Africa’s premier surf destinations. From mellow beach breaks perfect for beginners to powerful point breaks that challenge even seasoned pros, the Moroccan coast offers waves for every level.

Our surf guides cover everything you need to know: swell direction and period, optimal tides, seasonal wind patterns, and detailed breakdowns of each spot. Whether you’re planning your first surf trip or you’re a regular hunting new breaks, these in-depth guides will help you score the best waves Morocco has to offer.
