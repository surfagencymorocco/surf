Surf Equipment Guide Morocco — What to Bring & Rent

Packing the right gear can make or break your Morocco surf trip. The Atlantic coast demands specific equipment depending on the season, the spots you plan to surf, and your skill level. The good news? Most surf camps in Taghazout, Tamraght, and Imsouane offer high-quality rental equipment, so you don’t need to haul a full quiver across the airport.

This guide covers everything: recommended board types for Moroccan waves, wetsuit thickness by season (3/2 in summer, 4/3 in winter), what accessories to pack (reef boots, ear plugs, high-SPF zinc), and what you can comfortably leave at home and rent locally.
