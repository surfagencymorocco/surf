Surf Camp Morocco from Spain

For Spanish surfers, Morocco is practically on the doorstep. Direct flights from Madrid, Barcelona, Málaga, and Valencia to Agadir take just 2 hours — less time than a drive from Madrid to the Cantabrian coast. While Spain’s northern breaks are excellent, Morocco offers warmer water, more consistent swell, and lower prices. EU citizens enter visa-free, Spanish is widely understood in the south of Morocco, and our all-inclusive 8-day surf camps start from just 550 EUR.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Africa’s longest wave delivers rides up to 800 metres. Perfect for beginners, longboarders, and anyone who wants to enjoy long, cruisy sessions. Relaxed village atmosphere with incredible seafood.

Tamraght

Hillside Berber village with panoramic Atlantic views. Quiet and authentic, with easy access to excellent beach breaks at Banana Beach and Crocodile Beach. Great for all levels.

Taghazout

World-class right-hand point breaks just a short flight from Spain. Anchor Point, Killer Point, and Hash Point offer everything from mellow peelers to powerful barrels.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from Spain

How long is the flight from Spain to Agadir?
Direct flights from Madrid and Barcelona to Agadir take approximately 2 hours. Flights from Málaga and Valencia are similarly short, at around 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours. Morocco is also reachable by ferry from southern Spain (Algeciras to Tangier), though Agadir is a further 8-hour drive from Tangier.
Which airlines fly from Spain to Agadir?
Ryanair and Air Arabia Maroc offer direct flights from Madrid and Barcelona to Agadir. Royal Air Maroc also serves the route, and seasonal charter flights operate from various Spanish cities during peak holiday periods.
Do Spanish citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. Spanish (EU) citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Morocco for stays up to 90 days. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from Spain?
Our all-inclusive 8-day packages start from 550 EUR per person. Return flights from Spain to Agadir are among the cheapest in Europe, typically ranging from 50 EUR to 150 EUR. Total trip cost including flights is approximately 620–730 EUR, making Morocco the best-value surf destination for Spanish travellers.
