Surf Camp Morocco from Poland

Poland’s surf community is growing fast, and Morocco is the number-one destination for Polish surfers looking to escape the Baltic winter. Direct flights from Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, and Katowice to Agadir take around 5 hours, putting world-class waves within a half-day’s travel. EU citizens enter visa-free, English is widely spoken (and many locals speak French), and our all-inclusive 8-day surf camps start from just 550 EUR — incredible value that makes a week in Morocco cheaper than a week at many European surf destinations.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Africa’s longest wave — 800-metre rides on a gentle, peeling right. The perfect place for Polish first-timers and longboarders. Laid-back village with fresh seafood at the harbour.

Tamraght

Traditional Berber village with stunning ocean panoramas. Quiet, authentic, and perfect for surfers who want quality waves without the crowds. Close to Banana Beach and Crocodile Beach.

Taghazout

Morocco’s surf capital. Anchor Point, Killer Point, and Hash Point deliver consistent world-class waves. Vibrant cafes, rooftop terraces, and an international surf community.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from Poland

How long is the flight from Poland to Agadir?
Direct flights from Warsaw Chopin, Kraków, Wrocław, and Katowice to Agadir take approximately 5 hours. Some flights may route via a European hub, adding 1–3 hours to total travel time.
Which airlines fly from Poland to Agadir?
Ryanair operates direct flights from multiple Polish cities (Kraków, Warsaw Modlin, Wrocław) to Agadir, especially during the winter season. Wizz Air and LOT Polish Airlines also serve the route, sometimes with a short connection via European hubs.
Do Polish citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. Polish (EU) citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Morocco for stays up to 90 days. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from Poland?
Our all-inclusive 8-day packages start from 550 EUR per person. Return flights from Poland to Agadir typically cost between 80 EUR and 250 EUR when booked in advance. Total trip cost (flights + camp) is approximately 650–830 EUR, making it one of the most affordable surf trips available from Poland.
