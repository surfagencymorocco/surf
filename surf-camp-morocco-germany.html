Surf Camp Morocco from Germany

Germany has one of the strongest surf travel cultures in Europe, and Morocco is the top destination for good reason. Direct flights from Frankfurt, Berlin, Cologne, Düsseldorf, and Munich reach Agadir in approximately 4 hours — faster than a drive to the Baltic Sea and with infinitely better waves. EU citizens enjoy visa-free entry, English and French are widely spoken, and our all-inclusive 8-day surf camps start from just 550 EUR. No jet lag, warm water, and consistent Atlantic swell — it’s the perfect winter escape for German surfers.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Rides up to 800 metres on Africa’s longest wave. The gentle, predictable break at The Bay is perfect for beginners learning their first green waves and longboarders chasing endless noserides.

Tamraght

Quiet hillside village with sweeping ocean views. A short drive to Banana Beach, Crocodile Beach, and Devil’s Rock — varied breaks suitable for all skill levels.

Taghazout

World-class right-hand point breaks. Anchor Point works on any swell direction, Killer Point delivers powerful barrels on bigger days, and Hash Point is perfect for intermediates.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from Germany

How long is the flight from Germany to Agadir?
Direct flights from Frankfurt, Cologne-Bonn, and Düsseldorf to Agadir take approximately 4 hours. Flights from Berlin and Munich are typically 4 hours 30 minutes. All flights operate within the same time zone (Morocco is 1 hour behind Germany in summer, same time in winter).
Which airlines fly from Germany to Agadir?
Ryanair operates direct flights from multiple German airports including Frankfurt-Hahn, Cologne, and Berlin. Condor and Eurowings also offer seasonal direct routes, especially during the winter sun season (October–April).
Do German citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. EU citizens (including Germany) enjoy visa-free entry to Morocco for stays up to 90 days. Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from Germany?
Our all-inclusive packages start from 550 EUR per person. Return flights from Germany to Agadir typically range from 100 EUR to 300 EUR depending on the city and season. The total trip cost (flights + camp) is approximately 680–880 EUR, making it significantly cheaper than long-haul surf trips.
