Surf Camp Morocco from UK

UK surfers have been flocking to Morocco for decades — and for good reason. While the British winter delivers cold water, howling onshores, and 4°C dawn patrols, Morocco serves up consistent Atlantic swell, water temperatures of 16–21°C, and over 300 days of sunshine. Direct flights from London Gatwick, London Stansted, Manchester, Birmingham, and Bristol to Agadir take just 3 hours 30 minutes to 4 hours. UK citizens enjoy visa-free entry for up to 90 days, and our all-inclusive 8-day surf camps start from 550 EUR (£470 approx) — far cheaper than a week in Cornwall or Devon.

3 Destinations

Imsouane

Africa’s longest wave — rides up to 800 metres. The Bay is a longboarder’s paradise and the perfect spot for beginners. Laid-back fishing village, incredible seafood, stunning sunsets.

Tamraght

Traditional Berber surf village on a hillside above the Atlantic. Quiet, authentic, and close to excellent breaks like Banana Beach, Crocodile Beach, and Devil’s Rock. Far from the crowds.

Taghazout

Morocco’s legendary surf town. Anchor Point is one of the best right-hand point breaks on the planet. Killer Point, Hash Point, and Panoramas offer world-class waves for all levels.

FAQ — Surfing Morocco from the UK

How long is the flight from the UK to Agadir?
Direct flights from London airports (Gatwick, Stansted, Luton) to Agadir take approximately 3 hours 30 minutes. Flights from Manchester, Birmingham, and Bristol take 3 hours 45 minutes to 4 hours. Morocco is on the same time zone as the UK during winter (GMT), so there is no jet lag.
Which airlines fly direct from the UK to Agadir?
Ryanair operates direct flights from London Stansted, Manchester, and Birmingham to Agadir. easyJet flies from London Gatwick and Bristol. TUI Airways and British Airways also offer seasonal direct flights, especially during the winter sun season (October–April).
Do British citizens need a visa for Morocco?
No. UK citizens can enter Morocco visa-free for stays up to 90 days. You only need a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date. Post-Brexit, there has been no change to Morocco’s visa policy for UK nationals.
How much does a surf camp in Morocco cost from the UK?
Our all-inclusive 8-day packages start from 550 EUR per person (approximately £470). Return flights from the UK to Agadir typically cost between £60 and £250 depending on the season and how early you book. The total trip cost (flights + camp) is approximately £550–£750, significantly cheaper than a comparable surf camp in France, Spain, or Portugal.
