When to Come

Know Your Season,
Catch Your Perfect Wave

High Season

October — April
  • Powerful Atlantic swells, 1–4 metres
  • Ideal for intermediate & advanced surfers
  • Best variety of surf spots
  • Warm weather, 18–26°C
  • Wetsuit recommended (3/2mm)

Beginner Season

May — September
  • Smaller, gentle waves — perfect for learning
  • Warm water, no wetsuit needed
  • Long summer days — more surf time
  • Hot sunny weather, 25–35°C
  • Great for families & first-timers

A Typical Day

Life at Camp —
Sun, Surf & Soul

Every day at SurfAgencyMorocco is crafted for maximum surf time, proper rest, and unforgettable Moroccan moments.

7:00 AM
Sunrise Breakfast
Fresh Moroccan breakfast — mint tea, msemen, honey, argan oil & fresh fruit.
8:00 – 11:00 AM
Morning Surf Session
Guided surf with your coach at the best break of the day. Warm-up, drills, free surf.
12:30 PM
Lunch & Rest
Tagine, fresh salads, seafood — traditional Moroccan cuisine. Siesta time.
3:00 – 5:30 PM
Afternoon Session & Coaching
Second surf or video analysis — review your footage, improve your technique.
7:30 PM
Dinner & Chill
Rooftop dinner, stories from the lineup, sunset over the Atlantic. Pure magic.
